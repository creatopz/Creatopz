Questions

Before you post

How do I know a creator's numbers are real?

Every listed creator is checked before they go live in the directory — we look at engagement and content history, not just a follower count.

Can I talk to a creator directly?

Not until a match is finalised — we mediate applications and introductions ourselves so nothing gets lost or ghosted.

What if I don't get applicants right away?

We're an early, growing marketplace — we won't pretend otherwise. Your brief stays visible to every matching creator and we actively surface it, but timelines aren't guaranteed.

Is there any charge beyond the 15% fee?

No. No listing fee, no subscription — the 15% platform fee on your set budget is the only charge.
