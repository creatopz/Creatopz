Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a starter template, not final legal advice. Please have it reviewed by a lawyer, particularly for compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act), before relying on it.

1. What we collect

When you create an account or profile on Creatopz, we collect:

  • Account details: name, email address, password (encrypted, never visible to us in plain text).
  • Creator profile data: bio, category, social media links, follower counts, engagement rate, audience demographics, rates, profile photo.
  • Brand profile data: company name, website, logo, campaign briefs and budgets.
  • Usage data: pages visited, actions taken (e.g. applying to a campaign), collected automatically to help us run and improve the platform.

2. How we use it

  • To operate your account and show your public profile (if you choose to list it) to relevant brands or creators.
  • To match creators with campaigns and coordinate collaborations.
  • To communicate with you about your account, applications, or campaigns.
  • To improve Creatopz and keep it secure.

3. What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data to third parties. Sensitive contact details (email, phone, exact rates) are only shared with a matched brand or creator once a collaboration is being coordinated by our team — not shown publicly.

4. Where your data is stored

Creatopz is built on Supabase, which stores data on secure cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit, and Row Level Security policies restricting who can access what.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your profile information at any time from your dashboard. To request full account deletion, contact us using the details below.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies/local storage required to keep you logged in. We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Creatopz evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us on WhatsApp.
