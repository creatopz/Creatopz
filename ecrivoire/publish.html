Good to know

Before you send it

What are you looking for?

Anything genuinely finished and genuinely yours — fiction, poetry, essays, memoir. We publish only two or three original titles a year, so we're picky, not because your book isn't good enough to exist.

How long until I hear back?

Every submission gets read. If it's a pass, you'll hear within a month. If we're excited, expect a phone call, not an email.

What does publishing with you actually involve?

We edit, design, typeset and hand-bind a small first print run under our own Écrivoire Edition numbering — the same process as our classics, just built around your original text.

Do I keep the rights to my work?

Yes. We license a print run; you keep authorship and copyright. We'll always talk terms plainly before anything is signed.
