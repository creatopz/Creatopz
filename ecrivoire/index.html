03 — About the studio

Part bookstore,
part bindery.

Écrivoire Studios opened in a former print shop, and we never quite stopped acting like one. Downstairs is the shop floor — fiction, poetry, essays, art and design, staff-picked and hand-labelled. Through the back is the bindery, where we repair broken spines, rebind well-loved paperbacks, and teach a Saturday class in Coptic stitching.
