03 — About the studio

Part bookstore,
part bindery,
one purple cow.

Écrivoire Studios opened in a former print shop, and we never quite stopped acting like one. Downstairs is the shop floor — fiction, poetry, essays, art and design, staff-picked and hand-labelled. Through the back is the bindery, where we repair broken spines and rebind well-loved paperbacks. Everywhere else is Inkling, our purple cow, making sure this place is impossible to confuse with any other bookshop in the city.
