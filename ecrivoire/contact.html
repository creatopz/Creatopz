FAQ

Common questions

Can I reserve a book to pick up later?

Yes — call, email, or ask next time you're in. We'll hold a title at the counter for up to a week.

Do you offer local delivery?

Free delivery within the Lower Marsh Quarter on orders over $40. Everything else ships via courier at a flat rate.

Can you rebind a book I already own?

Often, yes. Bring it in and one of our bindery staff will take a look and quote a price and timeline on the spot.

How do I reserve a seat for an event?

Visit the events page and click "Reserve a seat" on any listing — no account needed.

Do you buy used books?

We do, in small batches, Tuesday through Thursday mornings. Bring a short list first if you have a large collection.
