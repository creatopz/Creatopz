FAQ

Common questions

Do you have a physical store I can visit?

Not yet — we're online-only while we build out the catalogue. A shopfront is the long-term plan, and the newsletter will be the first place we announce it.

Can I get notified when a sold-out edition restocks?

Click "Join the Waitlist" on any sold-out title's page, or ask us here — we email you the moment a second run is confirmed (not every edition gets one).

Can you rebind a book I already own?

Often, yes. Post it to our studio with a note on what you'd like, and we'll quote a price and timeline before starting anything.

How do I reserve a seat for an event?

Visit the events page and click "Reserve a seat" on any listing — most of ours are online for now, no account needed.

Can I request a book you don't stock?

Yes — that's what the Wishlist Wall is for. Post it, and other readers can upvote it too. Remember we can only print public-domain texts, not books still under copyright.

I've written a book — can I publish it with you?

Please do. Visit Publish With Us and send us a pitch — we read every submission ourselves.
